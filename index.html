Core Thesis

“Start Focused. Build for Scale.”

Healthcare cannot be transformed overnight by unbounded promises. IndraMed.ai applies targeted, clinically grounded AI where diagnostic bottlenecks create the highest patient vulnerability — creating rigorous validation loops that establish foundation for multi-organ scale. Expansion from India → Uzbekistan / Central Asia → International depends on evidence, regulation, partnerships and readiness.

1. Focused Clinical Impact

Prioritizing high-burden respiratory diseases, early thoracic triage, and tuberculosis screening where chest radiography is standard of care but expert radiological reading is severely constrained.

Rapid Triage & Pattern Detection

2. Beyond Mere Pixels

Computer vision alone is not enough. We combine image-level pattern analysis with structured clinical intelligence — suggesting guideline-adherent differential investigations, laboratory follow-ups, and risk stratification.

Structured Decision Support

3. Phased Regional Scale

Anchoring initially in India and Central Asia (Uzbekistan) to address verified regional shortages before expanding globally. Every step is governed by evidence, regulatory clearance, and local clinical readiness.

Evidence-Driven Expansion

PRIMARY CORRIDORTier 1 Target

India(South Asia)

India accounts for approximately 27% of global tuberculosis burden — 2.8 million incident cases, 2024, WHO Global Tuberculosis Report 2025. Under the National TB Elimination Program (NTEP), massive chest X-ray screening campaigns face severe radiologist shortages in peripheral and district healthcare centers.

Estimated Annual TB Burden~2.8M Cases (2024, WHO 2025)
Radiologist Density< 1 per 100,000 (MoHFW)
Primary OpportunityFrontline Triage at PHCs

Uzbekistan(Central Asia)

Uzbekistan and Central Asian nations represent high priority zones for drug-resistant pulmonary infections and digital health modernization. Active healthcare digitalization initiatives by the Ministry of Health provide optimal pilot ecosystems. MDR/RR-TB remains high regional attention per WHO Europe 2024-2025.

Architecture & Intelligence Stack

Dual-Engine Clinical AI Architecture

Engineered for interoperability in resource-constrained environments without compromising state-of-the-art vision or data privacy.

Automated Quality Control

Detects motion artifact, improper patient rotation, clipping of costophrenic angles, and incorrect radiation exposure before AI inference to prevent false positives.

Pre-Inference QA Layer

Multi-Scale Vision Backbone

Ensemble convolutional and vision transformer (ViT) architectures trained to segment thoracic zones, identify opacities, nodules, cavities, effusions, and cardiomegaly.

Deep Computer Vision

Structured Reasoning Agent

Synthesizes imaging heatmaps with WHO guidelines and NTEP triage protocols to generate structured clinician summaries and recommended confirmatory investigations.

Clinical Decision Support

Edge & DICOM Ready

Designed for lightweight containerized deployment on edge gateway devices in rural health clinics with zero dependable internet, as well as cloud PACS integrations.

Offline Edge Capable
